Single sign-on with SAML 2.0 (Enterprise) next to OpenID Connect: the assertion must be signed with the provider's certificate, issued by the configured entity ID, meant for optik and answer a request the same browser started (checked once, across instances). optik publishes service provider metadata. Group mapping, teams, account creation and the password setting work as with OIDC. Also fixes buttons in the provider form that submitted it.
