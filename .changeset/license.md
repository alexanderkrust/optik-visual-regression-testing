optik is now licensed under Apache-2.0. Editions and license keys: without a key optik runs as Community edition (up to 5 reviewers); Team and Enterprise keys are Ed25519-signed and verified offline. Admins install them under *License* or with `OPTIK_LICENSE`. A key unlocks releases built within its update period; trials end on their date. Problems and too many reviewers only show notices for admins — nothing is ever locked.
