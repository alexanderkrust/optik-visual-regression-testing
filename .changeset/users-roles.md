Multiple users and roles: instance roles (admin, member) and project roles (viewer, reviewer, maintainer). Admins invite people with a link (optionally straight into a project) and manage users under *Users*; maintainers manage project members in the new *Members* tab. Members only see their projects; the UI hides what a role may not do. Every review records who made it ("rejected by … on …"). Role changes and removed accounts take effect immediately. Existing users become admins.
